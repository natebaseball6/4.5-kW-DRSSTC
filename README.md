Description:

An ultra high power QCW DRSSTC that can also function in non QCW mode. Includes a modernized UD2.7c complete with MIDI modulation, Over current detection, and under voltage lockout. Remotely controlled via fiber optic cable and an Arduino or similar microcontroller based system. A dual full bridge will switch the output of the buck circuit into the primary tank circuit. Target resonant frequency in the vicinity of 400kHz for optimal spark shape and switching ability. A buck circuit will progressively ramp up the voltage to lengthen the plasma while maintaining a sword like appearance without branching.
